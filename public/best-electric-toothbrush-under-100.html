The $50–$100 Sweet Spot

Between $50 and $100 you go from entry-level cleaning to genuinely great brushing with multiple modes, better pressure sensing, longer batteries, and in some cases app connectivity. The category is more competitive in 2026 than it has ever been, with brands like Oclean pushing established players to offer more at lower price points.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth spending more than $50 on an electric toothbrush?

Yes, in the $50–$100 range the added money buys real convenience: more modes, longer battery, better pressure feedback. Beyond $100 you are mostly paying for app features and premium materials rather than cleaning power.

Which is better under $100, Sonicare or Oral-B?

The Sonicare 5700 edges the Oral-B iO Series 4 on battery life (21 vs 14 days). The Oral-B iO Series 4 edges the Sonicare on pressure guidance (LED ring vs LED light) and mode count (5 vs 2). Pick by what you value more.
