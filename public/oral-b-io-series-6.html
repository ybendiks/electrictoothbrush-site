The Oral-B iO Series 6 sits at the midpoint of the iO lineup, priced at around $160. It is the first iO model to add a built-in LED display, and it replaces the Super Sensitive mode from the iO Series 5 with a dedicated Gum Care cleaning mode. If you are coming from the iO5 and have been diagnosed with early gum disease or want the visual feedback of an on-handle display, the iO6 makes a compelling case for the upgrade.

Oral-B iO Series 6 Specifications

The iO6 runs on the same magnetic drive motor as every iO Series model — a system that drives the round brush head in micro-vibrating oscillating rotations at up to 40,000 micro-vibrations per minute. What sets the iO6 apart are its five cleaning modes and its monochrome LED display ring around the handle.

  • Price: ~$160
  • Modes: Daily Clean, Sensitive, Whitening, Intense Clean, Gum Care
  • Display: Monochrome LED ring (shows mode, battery, pressure)
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth, Oral-B app, Position Detection
  • Charger: Magnetic inductive charger
  • Battery: Up to 2 weeks per charge
  • Head: iO round oscillating head

What the Display Actually Adds

The LED ring on the iO6 handle does three things: it illuminates in the color of your current cleaning mode so you always know which mode is active, it shows battery life remaining, and it lights up in red when you are brushing too hard. On the iO5, pressure feedback is also available — but only in the Oral-B app, which means you need your phone nearby to see it. The iO6 puts that signal on the brush itself.

In daily use, this is a more useful feature than it sounds. Most people do not brush with their phone open to the app. The on-handle display means you get real-time pressure feedback even when the app is closed, which translates directly into gentler brushing habits and less enamel wear over time.

Gum Care Mode vs. iO Series 5

The iO5 has a Super Sensitive mode, designed for people with very sensitive gums or fresh dental work. The iO6 replaces this with a Gum Care mode — a slower, gentler oscillation pattern designed to stimulate gum tissue and improve circulation in the gum line. The distinction matters if you have been told by your dentist to focus on gum health. Gum Care is a proactive mode; Super Sensitive is a comfort mode. If your concern is gum recession or early gingivitis, the iO6 is the more clinically relevant choice.

If you simply have sensitive teeth and want the softest possible clean, the iO5 or iO4 remain the better options — they still have Sensitive mode, and you can pair any iO head with a soft-bristle brush head for extra gentleness.

Oral-B iO Series 6 vs. iO Series 5: Is It Worth $40 More?

The iO5 and iO6 share the same motor, app, Position Detection, and two-week battery. The iO6 adds the LED display and swaps one mode (Super Sensitive → Gum Care). Whether that justifies $40 depends on your needs. If you want gum health coaching in a mode rather than just the app, the iO6 delivers. If you simply want the best brushing performance and already plan to use the app for feedback, the iO5 gets you there for less.

Oral-B iO Series 6 vs. iO Series 7: Is the Color Display Worth $60?

The iO7 upgrades the monochrome display to a full-color LED display and adds a premium magnetic travel case in place of the iO6's basic travel pouch. Both models have identical modes, the same motor, and the same app experience. The color display on the iO7 shows richer feedback — different colors per mode are visually distinct and clearer to read at a glance. The magnetic travel case also charges the brush while you travel. For frequent travelers or those who want the clearest visual display, the iO7 is worth the extra cost. For home users who travel occasionally, the iO6 offers the same core performance.

Who Should Buy the iO Series 6?

The iO6 is the right choice for people who have been told by a dentist to improve their gum health, who want real-time pressure feedback on the brush itself (not just in the app), and who want the iO app experience without paying iO7 or iO9 prices. It is not the right choice for those prioritizing value (iO4 or iO5) or those who want the top-of-the-line experience (iO7 or iO9).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Oral-B iO Series 5 and iO Series 6?

The iO Series 6 adds a monochrome LED display and swaps the Super Sensitive mode for a dedicated Gum Care mode. Both have Bluetooth, Position Detection, and the same magnetic drive motor. The iO6 costs about $40 more at ~$160.

Is the display on the iO Series 6 worth it?

The iO6 display shows your current mode, battery level, and a brushing pressure indicator ring. If you find yourself confused about which mode you are in or want instant visual pressure feedback, it is a genuine quality-of-life improvement. If you never read brushing status while brushing, the iO5 display-free design saves $40.

Should I buy the Oral-B iO Series 6 or iO Series 7?

The iO Series 7 adds a color LED display and a premium magnetic travel case for about $60 more. If you travel frequently and want the richest visual display, choose the iO7. If you brush at home and rarely travel, the iO6 offers the same Gum Care mode and core features at a lower price.
