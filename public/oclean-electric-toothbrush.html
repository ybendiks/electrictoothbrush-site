Who is Oclean?

Oclean is a Chinese brand owned by Xiaomi's ecosystem. It makes sonic electric toothbrushes with a strong emphasis on AI coaching, long battery life, and touchscreen displays at prices that undercut Oral-B and Sonicare. In 2026 the brand has grown significantly in North America and Europe, particularly on Amazon where the X Ultra sits in the top 10 bestsellers in the electric toothbrush category.

Oclean X Ultra — Flagship ($189.99)

The X Ultra is Oclean's headline model. It features a 0.96-inch colour touchscreen on the handle for mode selection and battery status — the first non-Sonicare brush to offer this. Battery life is 40 days. The AI Plaque Detection system uses the handle's sensors to generate a post-brush score and coverage map via the Oclean app. At $189.99 it is priced well below the Sonicare 9900 Prestige Next-Gen ($379.99) with comparable headline features.

Specs: 42,000 vibrations/min | 40-day battery | Touchscreen | 8 modes | AI coaching app | 72g

Oclean X Pro Elite — Best Value ($89.99)

The X Pro Elite is Oclean's mid-range and offers the best value in the lineup. No touchscreen, but the Oclean app handles mode switching and coaching. 35-day battery, 5 modes, and magnetic charging. For under $100 it offers app-connected features that cost twice as much from Sonicare or Oral-B.

Oclean F1 — Budget Entry ($39.99)

The F1 is Oclean's entry-level model. No app, one mode, 30-day battery. At $39.99 it competes directly with the Oral-B Vitality Plus and undercuts the Sonicare 4100 by $10. The 30-day battery is its standout feature at this price.

How Oclean Compares to Sonicare and Oral-B

Oclean's main advantages are long battery life, lower price for app-connected features, and the unique touchscreen on the X Ultra. The trade-offs: smaller replacement head selection in physical retail stores (mostly Amazon), a newer brand with less long-term reliability data, and app quality that lags behind the Sonicare app in coaching intelligence.

For buyers who prioritise long battery, app coaching, and value, Oclean is now a legitimate alternative. For buyers who prioritise established brand trust, wide brush-head selection in stores, and proven longevity, Sonicare or Oral-B remains the safer bet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Oclean a good electric toothbrush brand?

Yes, particularly for value. Oclean offers long batteries and app coaching at prices below Sonicare and Oral-B. The trade-off is a newer brand with less retail presence and smaller brush-head selection.

Are Oclean brush heads available in stores?

Mostly online. Oclean replacement heads are widely available on Amazon but not typically stocked in physical drugstores or supermarkets. Factor in shipping time when planning replacements.

Does Oclean work in the US?

Yes. The X Ultra, X Pro Elite, and F1 all ship to the US and include a US-compatible charger. The Oclean app is available on iOS and Android in English.
