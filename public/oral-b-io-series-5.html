What App and Position Detection Actually Add

Most electric toothbrush apps are gimmicks. The Oral-B iO app is different in one specific way: Position Detection. The iO5's handle contains sensors that communicate with your phone to map your brushing in real time across six mouth zones — upper left, upper front, upper right, lower left, lower front, lower right.

After each brushing session, the app shows you a map of how long you spent in each zone, flagging zones you spent under 30 seconds in. Most people, even experienced electric toothbrush users, consistently under-brush the upper back molars on their dominant hand side and the lower front teeth. Position Detection catches this. Within two to four weeks of consistent app use, most people develop meaningfully better zone coverage that sticks even when they stop watching the app.

Beyond zone tracking, the app offers streak tracking (how many days in a row you hit your brushing goals), real-time brushing intensity feedback, and a brushing calendar. The streak tracking in particular drives habit consistency in a way that the raw timer does not. It connects to Apple Health and Google Fit for people who track health metrics broadly.

If you are the type of person who tracks workouts, steps, or sleep data — you will use this app and find it valuable. If you have zero interest in quantified self data for dental care, the iO4 gives you the same cleaning quality without the app overhead.

The Five Cleaning Modes

Daily Clean: Full iO power for standard twice-daily brushing. This is the mode for 95% of your brushing sessions.

Sensitive: Reduced intensity for sensitive teeth or gum line sensitivity. Common for people who experience temporary sensitivity after whitening treatments or cold weather.

Super Sensitive: Even gentler than Sensitive. Designed for post-dental procedure recovery, severe chronic sensitivity, or new users who find the standard Sensitive mode too intense during adjustment.

Whitening: Polish-focused mode for surface stain removal. Best used a few times per week rather than daily. Targets coffee, tea, and wine stains effectively. Results depend on stain type and existing enamel condition.

Intense Clean: The mode unique to iO5 vs iO4. Higher power setting for a deeper scrub when you want maximum plaque removal — after a long flight, before a dentist appointment, or when you have been skipping sessions. Not recommended for daily use; it is a boost mode.

iO Series 5 vs iO Series 4: Is App Worth $20?

The honest answer: if you will use the app, yes. If you will not, no. The iO Series 4 cleans identically to the iO5. The motor, the head, the pressure sensor, the battery life — identical. The $20 premium on the iO5 buys you Bluetooth, Position Detection, the app, and Intense Clean mode.

Position Detection is the feature that justifies it for habitual app users. Most people who try it find that they were consistently missing one or two mouth zones without realizing it. That kind of specific, actionable feedback is what the $20 premium delivers. The Intense Clean mode is a bonus for high-motivation brushing sessions, but most people could live without it.

iO Series 5 vs iO Series 6: Is the Display Worth $40?

The iO Series 6 costs approximately $160 — $40 more than the iO5 — and makes two changes: it replaces Super Sensitive with Gum Care mode, and it adds a small monochrome display on the handle that shows mode name and battery level.

Gum Care mode is a specific therapeutic mode designed for people with active gum issues, periodontitis, or recommended by their dentist for targeted gum stimulation. If your dentist has told you to focus on gum health, the iO6's Gum Care mode is the reason to upgrade. If gum disease is not an active concern, the iO5's five modes cover everything most people need and the handle display is a nice-to-have at best.

The iO5 is the better buy for the majority of buyers. The iO6 is for gum-health-focused users who can justify the extra $40.

Oral-B iO Series 5 vs Philips Sonicare 5700

The Sonicare ProtectiveClean 5700 is the closest Sonicare competitor at a similar price point (~$99–$119). Both have app connectivity, multiple cleaning modes, and pressure sensors. The fundamental technology difference remains: Oral-B oscillates and rotates (physical scrubbing of each tooth surface), while Sonicare generates sonic vibrations that create fluid dynamics cleaning between teeth.

Clinical studies consistently show both technologies are effective at plaque removal. The Oral-B iO5 has an edge in app features (Position Detection is more specific than Sonicare's coaching). The Sonicare 5700 has BrushSync brush head wear tracking. Both have similar battery life. If you are already in one brand's ecosystem (you use that brand's replacement heads), stay with that brand. If starting fresh, the iO5 edges out the Sonicare 5700 on app sophistication at a similar price.

Who Should Buy the iO Series 5?

The iO Series 5 is the right pick for most buyers who want an electric toothbrush in the $100–$150 range. It is our top recommendation overall because it combines the iO platform's core cleaning quality with genuinely useful smart features at the lowest price point where those features are available. At sale prices ($79–$89), it becomes an extraordinary value — you are getting better technology than most $150 brushes for the price of a budget model.

The only buyers who should skip it: those who are firmly opposed to using a smartphone app for tooth brushing (choose the iO4 instead), those with active gum disease who need Gum Care mode (choose the iO6), or those who want the ultimate brushing experience and premium build quality (iO7 or iO9).

Verdict

The Oral-B iO Series 5 is the sweet spot of the iO lineup. At ~$120 full price and frequently available for $79–$89, it delivers the iO motor, five cleaning modes, app connectivity with Position Detection, and the pressure sensor that trains better brushing habits. It is better than the iO4 if you will use the app; it is meaningfully cheaper than the iO6 and iO7 without sacrificing cleaning performance. For most buyers in 2026, this is the one to buy.
