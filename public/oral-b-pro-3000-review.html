At around $45, the Oral-B Pro 3000 is the most affordable Oral-B electric toothbrush with Bluetooth app connectivity. It delivers three cleaning modes, a visible pressure sensor, and the proven CrossAction round head — all for well under $50. If you want to enter the Oral-B app ecosystem without committing to the iO Series price range, the Pro 3000 is where to start.

Oral-B Pro 3000 Specifications

  • Price: ~$45
  • Modes: Daily Clean, Sensitive, Whitening
  • Pressure sensor: Yes (visible indicator)
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth, Oral-B app
  • Timer: 2-minute with 30-second quadrant pacer
  • Head: CrossAction (compatible with all standard Oral-B heads)
  • Battery: ~7–10 days per charge

Bluetooth on a $45 Brush

The most compelling thing about the Pro 3000 is that it brings app connectivity to a sub-$50 price point. The Oral-B app tracks which zones you have brushed, how long you spent on each quadrant, whether you pressed too hard, and builds a brushing history over time. For people who want to improve their brushing habits — coverage, timing, pressure — the app is a genuinely useful tool. The Pro 3000 is the entry ticket to that ecosystem.

Three Modes: Daily Clean, Sensitive, Whitening

Daily Clean is the standard oscillating rotation mode for everyday plaque removal. Sensitive runs at a lower intensity for people with tender gums or recently worked dental tissue. Whitening uses alternating rotation patterns to remove surface staining. Three modes cover the most common use cases. The main omission compared to the iO Series is Gum Care and Intense Clean modes — but for most people brushing twice daily, the three Pro 3000 modes are sufficient.

Pro 3000 vs. Pro 1000: Worth the Extra $20?

The Pro 1000 has one mode (Daily Clean) and no Bluetooth. The Pro 3000 adds Sensitive and Whitening modes and the Oral-B app. If you want to occasionally use a different mode or find the app motivating for habit building, the Pro 3000 is worth $20 more. If you plan to use Daily Clean exclusively and never open an app, save the $20 and get the Pro 1000.

Pro 3000 vs. Oral-B iO Series 3

The iO Series 3 ($90) represents the next generation of Oral-B technology. It uses a magnetic drive motor rather than the conventional electric motor in the Pro 3000, which makes it quieter, more responsive, and generates a different sensory experience. The iO round head also fits more closely between teeth. If the Pro 3000's CrossAction head works well for you, there is no urgent need to upgrade. But if you test-drive an iO Series brush, you will likely never want to go back — the difference in feel is noticeable. Think of the Pro 3000 as the best of the older generation; the iO3 as the entry point to the new generation.

Who Should Buy the Oral-B Pro 3000?

The Pro 3000 is for budget-conscious adults who want app coaching and multiple modes without spending iO Series prices. It is also the right choice for someone who wants to try the Oral-B app before committing to a $90+ brush. If Bluetooth habit tracking appeals to you but the iO5's $120 price tag does not, the Pro 3000 delivers the core app experience at less than half the cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Oral-B Pro 3000 compatible with the Oral-B app?

Yes. The Pro 3000 connects via Bluetooth to the free Oral-B app on iOS and Android. The app tracks brushing time per zone, shows coverage maps, monitors pressure, and tracks streak data. It is the most affordable Oral-B model with Bluetooth connectivity.

What is the difference between the Oral-B Pro 1000 and Pro 3000?

The Pro 3000 adds two cleaning modes (Sensitive and Whitening, vs. just Daily Clean on the Pro 1000) and Bluetooth app connectivity. Both have the same motor, pressure sensor, 2-minute timer, and CrossAction head. The Pro 3000 costs about $20 more and is worth it if you will use the app or benefit from multiple modes.

How does the Oral-B Pro 3000 compare to the iO Series 3?

The iO Series 3 (~$90) uses the newer magnetic drive technology — it is significantly quieter, has a different feel, and offers more precise oscillation. The Pro 3000 uses the older CrossAction head and motor. Both have pressure sensors and similar modes, but the iO Series brushing experience is noticeably more refined. The Pro 3000 delivers excellent results at a lower price; the iO3 is a premium upgrade in experience, not just specs.
