Quick Verdict

The SURI 2.0 is the best argument that a sustainable electric toothbrush does not have to compromise on performance. A 38-day battery, a UV-C travel case, compostable brush heads, and a genuinely quiet 58 dB motor at $134.99 make it one of the most compelling mid-range brushes of 2026. Electricteeth.com named it their Editor's Choice for sustainable brushing. We agree.

What's New in the SURI 2.0

SURI launched the original brush in 2022 as a design-forward, eco-conscious alternative to Oral-B and Sonicare. The 2.0, released in early 2026, addresses the original's main weaknesses: battery life and travel convenience.

The battery nearly doubles — from 21 days to 38 days — which means most people charge it once a month. The UV-C travel case is now standard rather than an add-on. It sterilises the brush head during storage using UV light and doubles as a charger via USB-C, eliminating the need to carry a separate charging stand. The handle sheds 3g to arrive at 78g, making it one of the lightest sonic brushes available.

Sustainability Credentials

SURI is a certified B Corporation — the same accreditation held by Patagonia and Ben & Jerry's. The brush heads use plant-based bristles and arrive in compostable packaging. The handle is designed for repairability and longevity. SURI also runs a brush head recycling program: you post back used heads and SURI recycles the materials. No other major electric toothbrush brand offers this at scale.

Cleaning Performance

Two modes is a genuine limitation compared to the eight offered by the Sonicare 9900 Prestige Next-Gen. In practice most people use one mode anyway, and the SURI's 31,000 strokes per minute is on par with the Sonicare 4100 and similar entry-to-mid-range sonics. Plaque removal in daily testing was thorough and gums felt clean. The absence of a dedicated gum-care or whitening mode will matter to some users and not at all to others.

The lack of a hard pressure sensor is worth noting. SURI changes the brush's vibration pattern when you press too firmly, which most users can feel, but there is no LED or app alert. If you tend to brush hard and need an explicit warning, the Sonicare 4100 or Oral-B iO Series 4 may suit you better.

Travel and Portability

The UV-C travel case is one of the SURI 2.0's strongest selling points. It is compact, charges via the same USB-C cable as most modern phones, and sanitises the brush head during storage — useful for travel or shared bathroom surfaces. The 38-day battery means weekend or even week-long trips need no charger at all.

Who Should Buy the SURI 2.0

The SURI 2.0 is the right brush if sustainability matters to you, you travel frequently, and you want a great brush without paying Sonicare flagship prices. If you need multiple cleaning modes, a hard pressure sensor, or Bluetooth coaching, look at the Sonicare 7100 or Oral-B iO Series 7 instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the SURI 2.0 battery last?

38 days on a single charge, based on brushing twice daily for two minutes. This is nearly double the original SURI's 21-day battery life.

Does the SURI 2.0 have a pressure sensor?

Not a dedicated LED or app alert. The brush changes its vibration pattern when you apply too much pressure. Most users find this sufficient, but it is less explicit than the light-based sensors on Oral-B iO or Sonicare models.

Is the SURI 2.0 worth it over the Sonicare 4100?

If sustainability, travel convenience, and the UV-C case matter to you, yes. The SURI 2.0 cleans comparably to the Sonicare 4100 but costs $86 more. The Sonicare wins on price; the SURI wins on eco-credentials and the travel case.

Can you recycle SURI brush heads?

Yes. SURI operates a mail-back recycling programme for used heads. Packaging is compostable. The bristles are plant-based nylon.
