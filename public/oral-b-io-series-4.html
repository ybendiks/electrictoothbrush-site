Oral-B iO Series 4 Review (2026)

Updated October 2026 — by the ElectricToothbrush.site editorial team

The Oral-B iO Series 4 is the model we most often recommend to buyers who want the iO platform without paying for app features they will never use. At approximately $100, it adds two meaningful upgrades over the base iO Series 3 — a hard travel case and the Super Sensitive cleaning mode — for about $10 more. Below the iO5 ($119), you get the same core iO cleaning performance and simply skip the Bluetooth app and Position Detection coaching. For most people, that is exactly the right trade-off.

Why the iO4 Hits the Sweet Spot

The iO Series lineup follows a clear feature progression: iO3 is the baseline, iO4 adds a travel case and Super Sensitive, iO5 adds app/Bluetooth/Position Detection and Intense Clean, iO6 adds Gum Care mode and better display, and iO7 adds a color display and premium magnetic travel case. Each step up costs $10–$50 more and adds specific features.

The iO4 captures the two most universally useful upgrades over the iO3 — travel case and Super Sensitive mode — at minimal additional cost. The travel case alone would cost $15–$20 purchased separately. Super Sensitive is genuinely useful for anyone who has had recent dental work, gum sensitivity flare-ups, or is transitioning a child to the iO platform. After the iO4, the next meaningful upgrade is the app at the iO5 level, which costs $20 more. For buyers who are confident they will not use the app, the iO4 is the natural stopping point.

The Four Cleaning Modes

Daily Clean: Full-power iO cleaning for twice-daily use. The mode that effectively removes plaque and the one you will use 95% of the time.

Sensitive: Reduced intensity for sensitive teeth or initial adjustment to electric brushing. Noticeably gentler on the gum line.

Super Sensitive: The mode exclusive to iO4 and above (vs iO3). Lower intensity than Sensitive. Designed for post-dental procedure recovery, severe gum sensitivity, or users with periodontal disease who need minimal gum stimulation. Also useful as a "starter mode" for children learning to use the brush.

Whitening: Polishing-focused mode that targets surface stains from coffee, tea, and wine. Best used a few times per week rather than daily, as heavy surface polishing every day is unnecessary and potentially abrasive over time.

Travel Case: The Underrated iO4 Advantage

The included hard plastic travel case is a genuine differentiator versus the iO3. It snaps shut firmly, protects the brush head from contamination during transit, and is compact enough to fit in a toiletry bag without dominating it. For frequent travelers — business trips, weekend travel, vacation — having the case included matters. You do not need to buy a separate case or pack the brush loose in your bag hoping the head stays clean.

The iO5 does not include a travel case at its standard price (it is sometimes included in bundle configurations). The iO7 includes a premium magnetic travel case. So in the iO lineup, the iO4 is uniquely positioned: it is the lowest-price model that includes a case standard in the box.

iO Series 4 vs iO Series 3: Is the Upgrade Worth It?

At normal retail pricing, yes — the iO4 is worth the extra ~$10. You get a $15–$20 travel case and the Super Sensitive mode. Unless the iO3 is significantly discounted (more than $20 below the iO4 price), the iO4 offers better value. On Black Friday, the iO3 sometimes drops low enough that the math changes; outside of sale periods, choose the iO4.

iO Series 4 vs iO Series 5: Is the App Worth $20?

The iO Series 5 costs approximately $20 more and adds three things: Bluetooth app connectivity, Position Detection (mouth zone mapping), and the Intense Clean mode. The question is whether you will actually use the app.

If you are someone who tracks workouts with a fitness app, uses streaks and habit tracking in other areas of your life, or has a specific dental goal (improving gum health, reducing plaque in a particular zone) — the iO5's app coaching is genuinely valuable. Position Detection catches brushing blind spots that even experienced brushers develop, particularly in the upper back molars.

If you have zero interest in connecting your toothbrush to your phone, the iO4 gives you identical cleaning performance for $20 less. The iO motor, the round head, the pressure sensor, the 14-day battery — all the same. You are purely deciding on app features.

Who Should Buy the iO Series 4?

Travelers: The included hard travel case makes the iO4 the natural choice for anyone who brushes away from home regularly. Pack it, protect the head, and the 14-day battery means you often will not need to bring a charger.

App-indifferent buyers: If you want the iO platform's cleaning quality without the phone connectivity overhead, the iO4 is the highest iO model you should consider. Beyond it, you are paying for app features.

Sensitivity-focused users: The Super Sensitive mode below the iO3's Sensitive adds meaningful flexibility for post-dental recovery or chronic gum sensitivity. Anyone who has had scaling and root planing, gum surgery, or similar procedures will benefit from Super Sensitive during recovery.

Buyers wanting the Oral-B round head at a reasonable price: The iO3 is the entry point, but the iO4 is the better value at normal retail. It is the lowest-cost way to get the full iO cleaning experience with practical accessories included.

Verdict

The Oral-B iO Series 4 is the recommendation for most buyers who want the iO platform without committing to app features. At ~$100, it beats the iO3 on value (travel case + extra mode for ~$10 more) and saves $20 over the iO5 for buyers who will not use the app. The iO motor delivers the same clinical cleaning quality as the rest of the lineup, the pressure sensor trains better habits, and the 14-day battery handles travel comfortably. It is the pick that requires the least amount of explaining.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Oral-B iO Series 4 the best value in the iO lineup?

The iO Series 4 offers the best balance of features and price in the iO Series lineup for most buyers who do not want app connectivity. Compared to the iO3, you get a travel case and Super Sensitive mode for ~$10 more. Compared to the iO5, you save $20 by skipping Bluetooth, the app, and Intense Clean mode. For travelers and simplicity-seekers, the iO4 is the sweet spot.

What cleaning modes does the Oral-B iO Series 4 have?

The Oral-B iO Series 4 has four cleaning modes: Daily Clean, Sensitive, Super Sensitive, and Whitening. The iO5 adds a fifth mode, Intense Clean, for a deeper scrub. The iO6 and above replace Super Sensitive with Gum Care.

Does the Oral-B iO Series 4 come with a travel case?

Yes. The Oral-B iO Series 4 includes a hard plastic travel case that holds the brush and a brush head. This is one of the key upgrades over the iO Series 3, which does not include a travel case.
