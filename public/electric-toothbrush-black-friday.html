When Do Black Friday Toothbrush Deals Drop?

The deal season now runs for most of November. Here is the typical timeline based on the past three years of Amazon and retailer data:

Mid-October (Amazon Prime Big Deal Days): Amazon runs its second Prime event each October. Toothbrush discounts here are usually 20–30% off — good, but not the deepest of the year. Worth buying if you can not wait.

Early November (Pre-Black Friday sales): Best Buy, Target, and Walmart start their "early Black Friday" promotions around November 1–7. These often match or come close to the Black Friday price. Sonicare and Oral-B both run brand-specific deals through their Amazon stores and direct sites.

Black Friday Week (November 24–29): This is when the biggest cuts land. Amazon historically runs Lightning Deals and Deal of the Day promotions on toothbrushes every day of Black Friday week. The deepest single-day discounts usually fall on Black Friday itself (Friday, November 28) and Cyber Monday (Monday, December 1).

After Cyber Monday: Some deals extend into early December as "Cyber Week" promotions. However, popular models and color options sell out, so waiting past Cyber Monday is risky for gift purchases.

Where to Find the Best Deals

Amazon: Consistently has the most competitive prices and the widest selection. Prime members get early access to Lightning Deals. Use Subscribe & Save on brush head refills for an additional 5–15% discount once you have chosen a model.

Best Buy: Often price-matches Amazon and occasionally runs exclusive bundle deals that include extra brush heads or accessories not available elsewhere. Good for in-store pickup if you need it immediately.

Target: Runs 20–30% off sitewide deals on Black Friday. Circle members get an extra 5% back. Prices usually match Amazon within a few dollars.

Walmart: Historically competitive on Oral-B models. Sometimes has exclusive colors or bundle configurations not listed elsewhere.

Direct from Oral-B or Sonicare: Brand sites often run their own Black Friday sales. Advantage: access to refurbished/open-box models at significant additional savings, and bundles with extra brush heads not available at retailers.

Buying Tips

Don't wait until Black Friday itself to research. Know which model you want before the deals start. Brush head compatibility matters — if someone in your household already uses Sonicare, stick with Sonicare so you can share heads. If they use Oral-B, stay in the Oral-B ecosystem. Second, check whether the deal is on the standard model or a bundle. A "deal" that adds a second brush head for $10 over the normal price of the brush alone is not a real deal. Third, register your new toothbrush immediately. Both Sonicare and Oral-B offer 2-year warranty extensions for registered purchases, turning the standard 2-year warranty into a 4-year one.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do electric toothbrush Black Friday deals start?

Electric toothbrush Black Friday deals typically start in mid-October on Amazon (Prime Big Deal Days) and ramp up through the first week of November. The deepest discounts historically land the week of Black Friday itself — November 25–29, 2026 — with Cyber Monday (November 30) occasionally offering an additional 5–10% off on leftover inventory.

Are Black Friday electric toothbrush deals worth it?

Yes — Black Friday is the single best time of year to buy an electric toothbrush. Sonicare and Oral-B routinely discount their mid-range models 40–50% off MSRP. A brush that lists at $120 all year can drop to $59–$79. If you have been waiting to upgrade or want to buy one as a gift, November is the ideal window.

What is the best electric toothbrush deal tier for Black Friday?

For $25–$50, look for the Sonicare 4100 (normally $49, drops to $29) or the Oral-B Pro 1000 (normally $49, often $29–$34). For $50–$100, the Oral-B iO Series 5 (normally $119) and Sonicare ProtectiveClean 5100 (normally $69) both see significant cuts. Above $100, the Oral-B iO Series 7 and Sonicare DiamondClean 9000 are the top targets.
