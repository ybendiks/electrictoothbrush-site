Quick Verdict

The Sonicare 7100 is the first model in the 2026 lineup to include Bluetooth app coaching. At $159.99 it is a meaningful step up from the 6000 in one specific way: if you want real-time feedback on your brushing technique, coverage maps, and AI-powered coaching, this is the entry point. If you do not care about app features, the 6000 gives you essentially the same cleaning for less.

App Features

The Sonicare app uses the handle's built-in motion sensors to map your brushing across your entire mouth. After each session it highlights zones you missed and shows how long you spent in each quadrant. Over time the AI adapts coaching to your patterns — if you consistently under-brush the upper left rear, it will specifically prompt you there.

The app also tracks mode usage, pressure warnings, and battery status. It is not mandatory — the 7100 works fine without a phone — but if you want to improve your technique, it is genuinely useful rather than gimmicky.

Specifications

  • Price: $159.99
  • Motor: 62,000 brush movements per minute
  • Cleaning modes: 4 (Clean, White+, Gum Care, Sensitive)
  • Intensity levels: 3
  • Battery: 14 days
  • Pressure sensor: Yes
  • Bluetooth: Yes, Sonicare app
  • App: iOS and Android

7100 vs 7300

The 7300 ($199.99) drops the app but adds a fourth intensity level and a longer 21-day battery. Paradoxically, if you care more about long battery life than app coaching, the 7300 is the better buy despite being listed higher in the lineup. The 7100 is for app users; the 7300 is for those who want maximum battery and modes without a phone dependency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Sonicare 7100 require an app?

No. The 7100 operates fully without the app. Bluetooth is optional — the brush has onboard controls for all modes and intensities.

Is the Sonicare 7100 worth it over the 6000?

Only if you will actually use the Sonicare app. The cleaning power is identical; you are paying $70 more purely for Bluetooth coaching. If that does not appeal to you, the 6000 is the better value.

What is the difference between Sonicare 7100 and 7300?

The 7100 has Bluetooth app coaching; the 7300 does not. The 7300 has a 21-day battery; the 7100 has 14 days. Counterintuitively, the 7300 may suit users who want a longer battery and more whitening focus, while the 7100 suits those who want app guidance.
