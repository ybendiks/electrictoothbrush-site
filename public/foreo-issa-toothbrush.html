FOREO ISSA 3 Review (2026): The Case for Silicone Bristles

The FOREO ISSA 3 is unlike any other electric toothbrush on the market. Where Sonicare uses 31,000 sonic vibrations per minute and Oral-B uses oscillating rotation, FOREO uses pulsing silicone — soft, non-abrasive, and fundamentally different in cleaning feel. At around $179, it is also one of the most expensive toothbrushes in its category. Here is an honest look at who it is right for and who should look elsewhere.

What Makes the FOREO ISSA Different

Every other mainstream electric toothbrush uses nylon bristles mounted on a plastic brush head. The FOREO ISSA 3 uses a hybrid head with soft medical-grade silicone along the outer edge and PBT polymer bristles in the center. This silicone-dominant head is hypoallergenic, non-porous (bacteria cannot penetrate the surface), and dramatically softer than any nylon option.

The cleaning mechanism is pulsing T-Sonic vibrations delivered through the silicone into your gum line. This feels completely different from sonic or oscillating brushes — gentler, almost massaging. People who have experienced gum sensitivity or discomfort with other electric toothbrushes often find the ISSA 3 significantly more comfortable.

Key Specifications

Price: ~$179. Head material: Hybrid silicone + PBT bristles. Intensity settings: 16 levels via the app. Battery life: 365 days per charge. Charging: USB-C. App: Yes, for intensity and mode customization. Head replacement: Annually (not every 3 months). Warranty: 2 years.

The 365-Day Battery: A Real Differentiator

One of the ISSA 3's most practical advantages is its 365-day battery. Sonicare models manage 14 days; Oral-B iO models manage about 2 weeks. The ISSA 3 charges once a year via USB-C, which means you can travel indefinitely without a charging cable. For frequent travelers, this is genuinely useful.

16 Intensity Levels and App Control

The FOREO app lets you set brushing intensity from 1 to 16 and access different brushing programs. This is the most fine-grained intensity control in the category — most brushes offer 2–5 intensity levels. For someone building up tolerance to electric brushing after dental work, or managing extreme sensitivity, the granular control is a genuine benefit.

Cleaning Performance: The Honest Assessment

Here is where the FOREO ISSA 3 faces its biggest challenge. The clinical literature on plaque removal consistently shows sonic toothbrushes (Sonicare) and oscillating-rotating brushes (Oral-B iO) outperform silicone alternatives for plaque and tartar removal. The pulsing silicone is excellent for gum stimulation and surface cleaning, but the mechanical action simply does not achieve the same plaque disruption as 31,000 sonic strokes per minute.

This does not make the ISSA 3 a bad toothbrush — it cleans teeth effectively and is worlds better than a manual brush. But if plaque removal is your primary concern, the Sonicare 4100 at one-sixth the price is a more effective tool.

Who the FOREO ISSA 3 Is For

People with extreme gum sensitivity: If every other electric toothbrush has caused discomfort or bleeding, the ultragentle silicone approach is worth trying. Many users who could not tolerate sonic brushes find the ISSA 3 comfortable.

Hygiene-conscious buyers: Non-porous silicone does not harbor bacteria the way nylon bristles can. If hygiene concerns about your brush head are a priority, the ISSA 3's material properties are genuinely superior.

Low-maintenance travelers: A 365-day battery and annual head replacement means minimal upkeep and nothing to forget when packing.

Who Should Look Elsewhere

If maximum plaque removal is your goal, choose a Sonicare or Oral-B iO. If you want app coaching and brushing maps, the Oral-B iO Series 5 is better equipped for that at the same or lower price. If budget matters, the Sonicare 4100 provides better value for dental health outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does FOREO ISSA clean as well as Sonicare or Oral-B?

Clinical data on silicone bristle cleaning performance is more limited than on sonic or oscillating brushes. For plaque removal specifically, sonic toothbrushes like Sonicare have stronger clinical backing. The FOREO ISSA excels for people with extreme sensitivity or specific hygiene concerns about nylon bristles, but it is not the optimal choice if plaque removal is your primary goal.

How long do FOREO ISSA brush heads last?

FOREO ISSA brush heads are designed to last approximately one year, compared to the 3-month replacement cycle for Sonicare and Oral-B heads. The silicone material does not fray like nylon bristles, so the head retains its shape and texture much longer. FOREO recommends replacing when you notice the brushing feel has changed.

Is the FOREO ISSA worth the price?

The FOREO ISSA 3 at around $179 is significantly more expensive than comparable sonic or oscillating brushes. The value proposition is specific: ultragentle silicone bristles for sensitive gums, year-long head durability, 365-day battery, and a distinctive hygienic advantage of silicone over nylon. If those specific benefits match your needs, it is worth it. For most people who want effective plaque removal, the Sonicare 4100 at $29 is a better investment.
